# Code of Conduct

Cpp Learn is a place to learn C++ together. Everyone participating in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces is expected to help keep those spaces welcoming and constructive.

## Our expectations

- Be respectful and patient with people at every experience level.
- Give feedback about the work, with specific suggestions when possible.
- Assume good intent while taking responsibility for the effect of your words and actions.
- Respect different backgrounds, perspectives, and approaches to learning.
- Keep comments relevant to the project and help others find a path forward.

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory remarks, threats, sexualized attention, sharing someone else's private information without permission, and repeated disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Reporting a concern

If you feel comfortable, you may tell someone that their behavior is unwelcome. You are not required to do so. To report a concern privately, contact the repository owner through the contact method listed on their GitHub profile. Include relevant links or screenshots and a brief description of what happened. Please do not post sensitive details in a public issue.

If the repository owner's contact method is unavailable, use GitHub's **Report content** feature on the relevant comment or account. The maintainer should add a dedicated private contact address here when one is available.

## How reports are handled

The repository maintainer will review reports as promptly as possible, protect private information as far as practical, and avoid sharing details beyond what is needed to address the situation. Depending on the behavior, the maintainer may provide a reminder, request an edit or apology, hide or remove content, close or lock a thread, or restrict participation. Serious or repeated behavior may result in a ban from project spaces.

The maintainer will apply these expectations consistently, including to their own conduct. GitHub's own policies and reporting channels also apply on GitHub.
